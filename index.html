This Is The Final Goodbye

What started as an idea became a production, a crew, a website, a movie, and an amount of chaos that absolutely should have required a permit. Somewhere along the way, it became more than a page or a joke or a project. It became a little era of its own.

So this is not an announcement for a sequel, a reboot, a surprise season two, or a suspiciously timed “we're back” post. This is the ending. On April 1, 2027, The Adventures of Gaylord will cease operations and this site will go dark.

Until then, the movie stays here. The colors stay obnoxiously bright. The animations keep spinning. The siren continues doing whatever it thinks its job is. And we get one last stretch of time to enjoy the absurd thing we made together.

Thank you for watching. Thank you for helping. Thank you for being part of the adventure.
